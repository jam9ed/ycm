How this works
We are 100% online.

The yard closed to the public in 2020 and never reopened. Boats sell through photographs, walkaround video and a phone call — which weeds out the tyre-kickers and leaves us time for people who are actually buying.

Every boat is checked and sea trialled.

A marine mechanic goes over it, then it goes on the water, before it goes anywhere near a truck.

Professionally delivered.

Anywhere in the United States, on a proper boat transporter, with international shipping possible. We do not ship freight.
